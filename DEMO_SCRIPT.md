# Demo video script (target: 2 minutes 40 seconds, hard limit 3:00)

Judges are not required to watch past 3 minutes, so the best moments come first. Record the screen with narration.
Use Chrome, close other tabs and notifications, set the browser zoom so text is easy to read, and record at 1080p.
No music unless you own it. Do not show any Amazon or Alexa logos.

**Before you press record:** start Hearth, open http://127.0.0.1:8765/, click **Reset demo data**, set **Demo time**
to **8:20 AM**, and do one practice run with the microphone so the browser has permission.

| Time | On screen | Say (in your own words, keep it simple) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The whole page: lamp on the left, phone on the right | "Meet Rose. She lives alone. Her daughter Priya lives far away and worries. Hearth lets Rose just talk, and lets Priya know she's okay." |
| 0:15 | Tap the lamp. Say **"Good morning."** | "Rose doesn't have to learn an app. She says good morning." (let the reply be spoken) |
| 0:35 | Say **"I took my morning pills."** Point at the tool panel, then the phone | "The assistant calls two tools on the Hearth MCP server, over real MCP. On Priya's phone, both doses turn to taken." |
| 1:00 | Say **"I took my vitamin D."** | "It's not time yet, so Hearth won't record it. A wrong 'yes' can't hide a real gap." |
| 1:15 | Say **"I feel a bit lonely."** Then **"Yes."** | "Rose says she's lonely. Hearth asks before telling Priya. She says yes, and Priya sees it on her phone. Nothing is shared without her say-so." |
| 1:45 | Say **"Can I take two metformin?"** | "Hearth will not answer dose questions. It sends her to a pharmacist or doctor and offers to tell Priya. That's a rule in the tools, not a hope." |
| 2:05 | Say **"I fell."** The phone turns red | "If Rose falls, Priya is alerted right away, and Rose is told to call emergency services, because Hearth can't." |
| 2:25 | Scroll the tool panel, then show the 7-day chart | "Everything comes from a real self-hosted MCP server over Streamable HTTP, with thirty automated tests. Any MCP assistant can plug in. Hearth is open source." |
| 2:40 | Hold on the title and repo link | "Hearth. Voice check-ins for the people we look after." |

## Tips
- If the microphone is awkward on the day, type the phrases or tap the phrase buttons; the tool panel and phone
  update the same way. Say in the voiceover that the assistant here is a simulator for Alexa+.
- Do one rehearsal first, then **Reset demo data** and record the real take.
- Upload to YouTube or Vimeo as **Public**, in English, and check it plays when you are logged out.
- Put the repo link and a one-line description in the video description.
